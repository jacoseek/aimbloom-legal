申请删除 AimBloom 账号
Delete your AimBloom account

生效 / Last updated: 2026-11-03

简体中文English

你可以通过邮件申请删除 AimBloom 账号及关联数据，无需重新安装应用或购买会员。

提交删除申请

  1. 使用你登录 AimBloom 的 Google 账号邮箱，发送邮件至 contact@pengqiai.com，主题为“AimBloom 账号删除申请”。
  2. 正文说明“请删除我的 AimBloom 账号及关联数据”。如果无法使用原邮箱，请说明情况，我们会通过支持流程核实归属。不要发送密码、验证码或完整购买凭证。
  3. 我们会回复核实并确认处理结果。这是由支持人员处理的删除申请，点击邮件按钮本身不会立即删除数据。
通过邮件申请删除

没有打开邮件客户端？请复制邮箱：contact@pengqiai.com

将删除哪些数据

经验证并处理后，删除 AimBloom 账号、目标、阶段和任务、访谈回答与规划草稿、进度和备注，以及关联的购买和额度记录。删除后，这些 AimBloom 数据无法恢复。也可在邮件中明确申请仅删除特定目标内容、保留账号。

会员与保留记录

删除账号不会自动取消 Google Play 的自动续费，也不等同于申请退款。请在 Google Play 订阅管理中管理续费。无需先取消订阅才能发送删除申请；支持人员可以协助说明相关影响。删除账号后，原账号绑定的会员可能无法自动恢复到新账号，请在删除前向支持人员了解影响。

如法律义务或未解决的支付争议要求保留特定记录，我们会在处理回复中说明具体类别、原因和期限。托管服务的备份/日志和 Google 独立保存的支付记录受各自保留安排约束。详情见隐私政策。

Request account and data deletion

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app or purchasing a membership.

  1. Email contact@pengqiai.com from the Google email address used for AimBloom. Use the subject “AimBloom account deletion request”.
  2. Ask us to delete your AimBloom account and associated data. If you cannot access that email, explain the situation so support can verify ownership. Never send passwords, verification codes or complete purchase tokens.
  3. Support will verify ownership and reply with the result. This is a manually processed request; opening the email link does not immediately delete data.

Deletion covers your AimBloom account, goals, stages, tasks, interview answers, plan drafts, progress, notes, purchase and allowance records. Deleted AimBloom data cannot be restored. You may instead specify particular goal content to delete while keeping the account.

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel Google Play renewal or request a refund. Manage renewal in Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation is not required to submit a deletion request. A membership bound to the deleted account may not automatically restore to a new account; contact support before deletion about the effect.

If legal obligations or an unresolved payment dispute require retention, our response will explain the records, purpose and period. Provider backups/logs and Google transaction records follow their own retention arrangements. See the privacy policy.
