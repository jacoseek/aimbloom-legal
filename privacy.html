AimBloom 如何处理你的信息

本政策适用于 AimBloom Android 应用（包名 com.aimbloom.app）及本支持网站。隐私联系邮箱：contact@pengqiai.com。

1. 我们处理的信息及用途

  • 登录资料：使用 Google 登录时，我们通过 Google 和 Supabase 获取账号标识、邮箱、显示名称及可用的头像地址，用于建立账号、显示个人资料和识别你的数据。我们不获取 Google 密码、邮箱邮件或通讯录。
  • 你创建的内容：目标、访谈回答、规划草稿、阶段、任务、日期、进度和备注保存在云端，用于提供规划、编辑和进度记录功能。
  • AI 请求：当你发起 AI 规划时，相关目标、必要的访谈回答和规划上下文会通过服务器发送给 ToAPIs 及其提供的模型服务，以生成计划。显示名称、邮箱和头像不作为规划提示词发送。请勿在目标或回答中输入密码、支付资料或不必要的敏感个人信息。
  • 购买与额度：我们处理 Google Play 商品标识、订单标识、购买凭证、购买/到期/退款状态，以及免费目标和 AI 额度记录，用于核验、恢复会员、防止重复或欺诈交易。Google Play 处理支付卡和结账资料，AimBloom 不保存银行卡号。
  • 技术与支持信息：服务提供商可能处理 IP 地址、请求时间、设备或应用版本及错误日志，以保障运行和排查故障。你联系支持时，我们处理你提供的邮箱和沟通内容。本应用未接入广告或用于营销画像的分析 SDK。

2. 服务提供商与跨境处理

Google 提供登录与 Play 结算；Supabase 托管账号、数据库和后端；ToAPIs 及其模型服务处理你主动提交的 AI 规划内容。服务可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理信息。AimBloom 不出售个人信息，不把目标内容用于定向广告。AI 服务提供商按其适用条款和隐私实践处理请求；请在提交前评估内容的敏感性。

本支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，无额外广告或分析脚本；GitHub 可能按其政策处理访问日志。参考：Google 隐私政策、Supabase 隐私政策、GitHub 隐私声明。

3. 保存、保护和删除

账号及目标内容在你使用服务期间保存，直至你请求删除账号或相关内容；归档目标只是隐藏目标，不是删除。会员与额度记录用于履约、恢复购买和防欺诈。网络传输使用 HTTPS，应用在设备安全存储中保存登录会话；任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。

你可以按账号与数据删除说明申请删除。验证归属后，我们删除 AimBloom 账号、目标、任务、访谈/规划内容、进度记录和与该账号关联的购买/额度记录。如依法必须保留特定记录，或存在未解决的支付争议，我们会在处理回复中说明保留的类别、原因及期限。服务提供商可能按其备份和日志保留安排完成后续清除；Google 自行保存的交易记录受其政策约束。

4. 你的选择

你可编辑目标、停止提交 AI 请求、退出登录，并通过联系邮箱申请访问、更正或删除个人数据。Google 登录授权也可在 Google 账号设置中撤销。删除 AimBloom 账号不会自动取消 Google Play 订阅；请在 Google Play 中单独管理续费。无需先购买会员才能申请删除。

5. 年龄与政策更新

AimBloom 面向能够独立管理个人目标与账号的用户，不面向 13 岁以下儿童。若发现儿童未经适当许可提供的信息，请联系我们处理。政策发生变化时，我们会更新本页日期；重大变化将通过适当方式通知。

How AimBloom handles information

This policy applies to the AimBloom Android app (com.aimbloom.app) and this support website. Contact: contact@pengqiai.com.

Information and purposes

  • Account: Google and Supabase provide your account identifier, email, display name and available profile image URL for sign-in, your profile and access to your data. We do not receive your Google password, email messages or contacts.
  • Your content: Goals, interview answers, planning drafts, stages, tasks, dates, progress and notes are stored in the cloud to provide planning and progress features.
  • AI processing: When you request AI planning, relevant goals, interview answers and plan context are sent through our server to ToAPIs and its model service. Your profile name, email and image are not added to planning prompts. Do not enter passwords, payment information or unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Purchases: Google Play product and order identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase/expiry/refund status and usage allowances support verification, restoration and fraud prevention. Google handles payment details; AimBloom does not store payment card numbers.
  • Operations and support: Providers may process IP addresses, request times, app/device details and error logs for service operation and troubleshooting. Support correspondence includes information you choose to send. The app has no advertising or marketing analytics SDK.

Providers and international processing

Google provides sign-in and Play billing. Supabase hosts authentication, cloud data and backend functions. ToAPIs and its model service process AI planning requests. Processing may take place outside your country. AimBloom does not sell personal information or use goal content for targeted advertising. AI providers process requests under their applicable terms and privacy practices. Consider the sensitivity of content before submitting it.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. We add no advertising or analytics scripts; GitHub may process access logs under its privacy statement. Links to Google, Supabase and GitHub policies appear above.

Retention, security and deletion

Account and goal content is retained while you use the service, until you request deletion of your account or relevant data. Archiving is not deletion. Purchase and allowance records support access, restoration and fraud prevention. Connections use HTTPS and app sessions use device secure storage. No system is absolutely secure.

Follow the account deletion instructions to request removal. After ownership verification we remove your AimBloom account and associated goals, tasks, interview/planning content, progress, purchase and allowance records. If law or an unresolved payment dispute requires specific records to be retained, our response will identify the records, reason and retention period. Providers may clear residual backups and logs according to their retention arrangements. Google's transaction records are governed by Google's policies.

Your choices and changes

You may edit your content, stop AI requests, sign out or request access, correction and deletion by email. Google authorization can be revoked in your Google account. Deletion does not automatically cancel a Play subscription; manage renewals in Google Play. You do not need a paid membership to request deletion.

AimBloom is not directed at children under 13. Contact us about information provided by a child without appropriate permission. We update the date on this page when the policy changes and provide appropriate notice for material changes.
