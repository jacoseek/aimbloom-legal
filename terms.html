服务与 AI 计划

AimBloom 提供个人目标规划、任务编辑和进度记录，需要 Google 登录和网络连接。AI 建议可能有误，请自行检查并调整；不能替代医疗、法律、财务等专业建议。不要上传无权使用的内容或利用服务侵害他人权益。

免费目标和 Pro 会员

每个账号累计可免费创建 5 个成功规划或直接创建的目标。未完成草稿暂占名额；已成功创建的目标在完成、归档或删除后不返还免费次数。免费额度用完或订阅到期后，已有目标仍可使用和记录。

Pro 解除 5 个目标的创建限制，每个 UTC 自然月含 20 个完整成功 AI 计划；必要访谈和补问属于同一完整计划，技术失败不扣成功计划额度。额度按月刷新，不累积，永久会员适用相同额度，并非无限 AI。

首发永久会员仅面向首发早期用户，是一次性购买，无需续费。首发活动结束后将永久停售，后续不再推出买断方案，已购买的会员权益保留。未公布结束日期前，不使用虚构截止时间。买断并不自动取消你已有的订阅，请自行在 Google Play 管理续费。

Service and AI suggestions

AimBloom provides personal goal planning, editable tasks and progress records. Google sign-in and an internet connection are required. AI suggestions can be wrong: review and adapt them. They do not replace medical, legal or financial advice. Only submit content you are entitled to use and do not misuse the service.

Free allowance and Pro

Each account may cumulatively create 5 successfully planned or directly created goals for free. Unfinished drafts temporarily reserve capacity. Completing, archiving or deleting a successful goal does not replenish the allowance. Existing goals remain usable when the free allowance is exhausted or a subscription expires.

Pro removes the 5-goal creation limit and includes 20 complete successful AI plans per UTC calendar month. Necessary interview questions and follow-ups belong to the same plan. Technical failures do not consume a successful-plan allowance. Allowances reset monthly and do not roll over. Lifetime membership has the same allowance, not unlimited AI.

Payments and launch offer

US base prices are US$9.99 monthly, US$29.99 annually and US$59.99 once for Founder Lifetime. The Google Play checkout shows the applicable local currency, price, taxes and billing period. Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically until canceled in Google Play. Access continues for the paid period after cancellation. Refunds follow applicable law and Google Play procedures.

Founder Lifetime is a launch-only benefit for early users, purchased once without renewal. When the launch offer closes, the buyout will be permanently withdrawn and will not be offered again. Existing owners keep their membership. No end date is claimed until announced. Buying lifetime does not cancel a previous subscription; manage its renewal in Google Play.
